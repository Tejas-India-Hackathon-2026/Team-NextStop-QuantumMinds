Fraud Prevention Pipeline

SecureFlow-AI analyzes multiple signals before approving a payment.

01. User Behaviour

The system compares the current payment with the user's historical behaviour.

02. Transaction Amount

Unusual payment amounts are identified and assigned additional risk.

03. Recipient Analysis

Unknown or suspicious recipients increase the transaction risk score.

04. Device Analysis

New or suspicious devices are analyzed before payment approval.

05. AI Risk Score

All signals are combined to generate a risk score from 0 to 100.

06. Final Decision

The system generates ALLOW, ALERT or BLOCK based on the final risk.
